Website privacy

Traffic analytics

This academic website uses limited traffic analytics to understand readership and improve how research is presented.

Information recorded

For page views and selected interactions—such as opening a research video, selecting its DOI, downloading the CV, or opening the visitor map—the site records the time, event, page path, referral domain, IP address, and network-derived country, region, city, postal code, approximate coordinates, timezone, and network organization.

Accuracy and purpose

Network-derived locations are estimates. They may identify an internet provider, university gateway, mobile network, or VPN rather than a visitor’s actual city, and they do not identify a street address or precise physical location. The data are used only for audience measurement, basic security, and improving this website.

Retention and access

Raw IP addresses are available in the private owner export for no more than 30 days. An hourly cleanup removes raw IPs after that period; a monthly pseudonymous identifier and approximate geographic fields may remain for aggregate analysis. The site does not send its analytics events or load its visitor-counter map when a browser enables Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track. The data are not sold or used for advertising.

External services

The public visitor map is provided by Flag Counter, legacy aggregate counters are provided by visitor-badge, and the complete research video is hosted by TIB AV-Portal. Those services receive connection data when their content is loaded or opened and apply their own privacy terms.

Questions or removal requests

For questions about these records or a request concerning your data, email wz2481@columbia.edu.
