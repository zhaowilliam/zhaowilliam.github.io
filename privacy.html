Website privacy

Traffic analytics

This academic website uses limited traffic analytics to understand readership and improve how research is presented.

Information recorded

For page views and selected interactions, the site records the time, event, page path, referral domain, IP address, and network-derived country, region, city, postal code, approximate coordinates, timezone, and network organization. Interactions include selecting a paper, CV, contact or external link; viewing a page section; scroll milestones; and opening or playing research media.

Additional limited information includes the clicked link's public label and destination (without query strings or fragments), its page position and research topic, broad device/browser categories, screen-width ranges, foreground page time, local video playback milestones, page-load duration, and limited resource-loading failures. Only the named channel tags utm_source, utm_medium, and utm_campaign are accepted, in a restricted label format; arbitrary URL parameters are not recorded.

A random, temporary identifier in this tab's session storage groups actions within a visit. It expires after 30 minutes without recorded activity or after eight hours, and is not a persistent cross-visit identity. If session storage is unavailable, grouping may be limited to the current page. The site does not record form contents, keystrokes, mouse movements, screen recordings, or browser fingerprints.

Accuracy and purpose

Network-derived locations are estimates. They may identify an internet provider, university gateway, mobile network, or VPN rather than a visitor’s actual city, and they do not identify a street address or precise physical location. The data are used only for audience measurement, basic security, and improving this website.

Clicks do not confirm that a document was saved or read, or that an email was sent. Foreground time and scroll position do not prove attention. Playback milestones apply only to the locally hosted teaser; opening the embedded TIB video is not evidence that it was watched. Session counts are not counts of people.

Retention and access

Individual activity reports and CSV exports require the owner's private token. Raw IP addresses and temporary visit identifiers are available for no more than 30 days; an hourly cleanup removes them after that period, and exports suppress expired values even before cleanup. Event records, a monthly IP-derived pseudonymous identifier, and approximate geographic fields may remain for analysis. These data are not sold or used for advertising.

The site does not send analytics events when a browser enables Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track. The owner dashboard also provides an optional browser-exclusion switch for testing; it stores only an on/off preference locally, not a visitor identifier. Local previews do not send analytics.

External services

Legacy aggregate counters are provided by visitor-badge, and the complete research video is hosted by TIB AV-Portal. Those services receive connection data when their content is loaded or opened and apply their own privacy terms.
